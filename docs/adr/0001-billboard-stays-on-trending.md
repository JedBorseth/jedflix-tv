# Billboard stays on the Trending shelf

The Billboard is driven by that section's Trending Shelf (Trending Now / Trending Movies / Trending Shows), not by editorial or Provider Shelves.

On Home, Trending Now is pinned as the first rail and is omitted from the Home shelves editor. On Movies and Shows, editorial and Provider Shelves are still prepended before Trending; a future reader will see Jed's Movies as the first Movies rail and assume it owns the hero — that is deliberate for those sections.
